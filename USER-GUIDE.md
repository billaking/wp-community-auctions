## 🚀 Getting Started

### What You'll Need

- **WordPress Account**: You must be logged in to bid or create auctions
- **Valid Email**: Used for notifications and communication
- **Web Browser**: Works on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (desktop & mobile)

### First Steps

1. **Create an Account** (if you don't have one):
   - Click "Register" or "Sign Up" on your site
   - Fill in your details (username, email, password)
   - Verify your email address

2. **Log In**:
   - Click "Log In" 
   - Enter your credentials
   - You're ready to participate!

## 🛍️ For Buyers - How to Bid

### Finding Auctions

#### Browse All Auctions
1. Navigate to the **Auctions** page (usually in your site's main menu)
2. You'll see a grid of active auctions with:
   - Item photos
   - Current bid
   - Time remaining
   - Number of bids
   - Status badge (Active, Upcoming, Ended)

#### Filter by Category
At the top of the auction page, you'll see category pills:

```
✨ All Auctions (45)  📦 Electronics (12)  📦 Collectibles (8)  📦 Art (5)
```

- Click any category to filter auctions
- The active category is highlighted in purple
- Number in each badge shows available auctions

#### Search for Specific Items
- Use the search bar (if available) to find specific items
- Filter by categories to narrow down options

---

### Placing a Bid - Step by Step

**Example: Bidding on a Vintage Camera**

#### Step 1: View the Auction
1. Click on an auction card that interests you
2. You'll see the single auction page with:
   - **Photo Gallery**: Click thumbnails to view different angles (Top, Front, Left, Right, Back)
   - **Categories**: Tags showing what type of item it is
   - **Current Bid**: The highest bid so far
   - **Time Remaining**: Countdown to auction end
   - **Description**: Full details about the item

#### Step 2: Review the Details

Before bidding, check:

| Detail | What to Look For |
|--------|------------------|
| **Current Bid** | Starting point for your bid |
| **Minimum Bid** | Lowest amount you can bid |
| **Bid Increment** | Minimum increase over current bid |
| **Time Left** | How long until auction closes |
| **Photos** | Condition and appearance from all angles |
| **Description** | Detailed information, condition, features |
| **Seller** | Who's selling the item |

#### Step 3: Place Your Bid

1. Scroll to the **"Place Your Bid"** section
2. You'll see a bid form with:
   ```
   Bid Amount: [$___.__]
   Minimum bid: $125.00
   
   [Place Bid]
   ```

3. **Enter your bid amount**:
   - Must be equal to or greater than the minimum bid shown
   - Example: Current bid is $120, increment is $5, so minimum is $125

4. **Click "Place Bid"**:
   - The page will process your bid
   - You'll see a success message: *"Bid placed successfully! You are now the highest bidder."*
   - The current bid updates automatically
   - You'll receive a confirmation email

#### Step 4: Track Your Bid

After bidding:
- **Green Success Message**: Your bid was accepted
- **Updated Display**: Current bid now shows your amount
- **Email Confirmation**: Check your inbox for bid confirmation
- **Dashboard Update**: Bid appears in your "My Bids" section

---

### Using Buy Now (Instant Purchase)

Some auctions offer a **Buy Now** option for instant purchase.

**Example: Buying a Laptop Instantly**

1. **View the Auction**: Look for the Buy Now section below the bid form
2. **See the Price**: 
   ```
   Or buy it now for $899.00
   [Buy Now]
   ```
3. **Click "Buy Now"**: A confirmation dialog appears
4. **Confirm Purchase**: 
   - Dialog: *"Are you sure you want to buy this item now?"*
   - Click **OK** to confirm
5. **Success**: 
   - Auction ends immediately
   - Item is yours
   - You receive a purchase confirmation email
   - Seller receives notification to contact you

**When to Use Buy Now:**
- ✅ You want the item immediately
- ✅ The price is fair and you don't want to risk being outbid
- ✅ Auction is competitive and time is running out
- ❌ Don't use if you want to try bidding lower first

---

### What Happens When You're Outbid

**Example Scenario:**

1. **You bid $150** on a camera
2. **Someone else bids $160**
3. **You receive an email**: 
   > Subject: You've Been Outbid!
   > 
   > Someone has outbid you on "Vintage Camera - Excellent Condition"
   > Your bid: $150.00
   > Current bid: $160.00
   > 
   > [View Auction and Bid Again]

4. **Your Options**:
   - **Bid Again**: Place a higher bid to regain the lead
   - **Let It Go**: If the price is too high, let it pass
   - **Use Buy Now**: If available and price is acceptable

---

### Winning an Auction

**Example: You Win the Camera!**

#### What Happens When You Win

1. **Auction Timer Expires**: Time remaining hits 0:00:00
2. **You're the Highest Bidder**: No one outbid you in time
3. **You Receive "Winner" Email**:
   ```
   🎉 Congratulations! You Won the Auction!
   
   Item: Vintage Camera - Excellent Condition
   Your winning bid: $175.00
   Seller: John Smith (john@example.com)
   
   Next Steps:
   - The seller will contact you within 24 hours
   - Arrange payment and shipping
   - Complete the transaction
   ```

4. **Check Your Dashboard**:
   - Go to **My Bids**
   - Look for auctions with **"Won"** badge
   - See seller contact information

#### After Winning

1. **Wait for Seller Contact**: Seller has your email from the notification
2. **Arrange Payment**: Discuss payment method (PayPal, bank transfer, etc.)
3. **Arrange Shipping/Pickup**: Decide on delivery method
4. **Complete Transaction**: Pay and receive your item
5. **Leave Feedback**: (if your site has a review system)

## 📦 For Sellers - Creating Auctions

### Creating Your First Auction (Admin Backend)

**Example: Selling a Vintage Watch**

#### Step 1: Access the Admin Panel

1. Log in to WordPress admin
2. Navigate to **Auctions** → **Add New**

#### Step 2: Add Basic Information

1. **Title**: Enter a clear, descriptive title
   ```
   Example: "Vintage Rolex Submariner - 1965 - Excellent Condition"
   ```
   
   **Tips for Great Titles:**
   - ✅ Include brand, model, year
   - ✅ Mention condition
   - ✅ Be specific and accurate
   - ❌ Don't use ALL CAPS
   - ❌ Avoid excessive punctuation!!!

2. **Description**: Write a detailed description in the editor
   ```
   Example:
   
   Stunning 1965 Rolex Submariner in excellent condition. This vintage
   timepiece has been professionally serviced and keeps perfect time.
   
   Features:
   • Automatic movement
   • Original dial and hands
   • Recent service (2024)
   • Water resistant to 200m
   • Includes original box and papers
   
   Condition Notes:
   Minor scratches on case (normal wear)
   Crystal is scratch-free
   All functions work perfectly
   
   This is a rare opportunity to own a piece of horological history!
   ```

#### Step 3: Upload Photos (Up to 5)

The **Item Photos** section appears below the description:

```
📷 Top View      📷 Front View    📷 Left Side
📷 Right Side    📷 Back View
```

**Photo Guidelines:**

1. **Take Quality Photos**:
   - Good lighting (natural light is best)
   - Clear, in-focus images
   - Clean background (white or neutral)
   - Show item from all angles

2. **Upload Each Photo**:
   - Click **Upload** under each label
   - Select image from your computer or WordPress media library
   - Preview appears in the box
   - Click **Change** to replace or **Remove** to delete

3. **Recommended Views**:
   - **Top View**: Bird's eye perspective
   - **Front View**: Main face of the item
   - **Left/Right Side**: Profile views
   - **Back View**: Rear details, markings, serial numbers

**Why 5 Photos Matter:**
- Buyers can see condition from all angles
- Reduces questions and uncertainty
- Increases trust and bids
- Shows you have nothing to hide

#### Step 4: Set Auction Details

Scroll to the **Auction Details** meta box:

1. **Start Date & Time**:
   ```
   Format: 2025-12-07 10:00:00
   ```
   - Click the field to use date/time picker
   - Set when auction begins (can be future date)

2. **End Date & Time**:
   ```
   Format: 2025-12-14 22:00:00
   ```
   - Set when auction closes
   - Typical durations: 3, 5, 7, or 10 days

3. **Starting Price**:
   ```
   $500.00
   ```
   - Set your minimum starting bid
   - Price it to attract bidders but protect your value
   - Research similar items

4. **Reserve Price** (Optional):
   ```
   $1,200.00
   ```
   - Minimum price you'll accept
   - Hidden from buyers
   - If not met, you're not obligated to sell

5. **Buy Now Price** (Optional):
   ```
   $2,500.00
   ```
   - Instant purchase option
   - Usually 20-30% above expected final bid
   - Removes auction from listings when used

6. **Minimum Bid Increment**:
   ```
   $25.00
   ```
   - Minimum amount each bid must increase
   - Higher for expensive items
   - Typical: $1, $5, $10, $25, $50, $100

7. **Auction Status**:
   - **Upcoming**: Not yet started
   - **Active**: Currently running
   - **Ended**: Closed
   - **Cancelled**: Removed from sale

#### Step 5: Add Categories and Tags

On the right sidebar:

1. **Categories**: Check relevant categories
   ```
   ☑ Watches
   ☑ Vintage Items
   ☑ Luxury Goods
   ```

2. **Tags**: Add searchable keywords
   ```
   rolex, submariner, vintage, luxury, collectible
   ```

#### Step 6: Publish

1. Click **Publish** button (top right)
2. Your auction is now live!
3. View it on the frontend to confirm everything looks good

### Creating an Auction (Frontend Dashboard)

**Example: Selling Collectible Comics**

Users can create auctions without accessing WordPress admin.

#### Step 1: Access Dashboard

1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to the **Dashboard** page
3. Click **Create New Auction** button

#### Step 2: Fill Out the Form

You'll see a user-friendly form with clear instructions at the top.

1. **Auction Title**:
   ```
   Marvel Comics Collection - Amazing Spider-Man #1-50
   ```

2. **Description**:
   ```
   Complete run of Amazing Spider-Man issues #1-50 from the 1960s.
   
   All comics are in VG to NM condition, professionally graded.
   This is a rare complete collection perfect for serious collectors.
   
   Included:
   - Issue #1 (first appearance, graded VG 4.0)
   - Issues #2-50 (various grades VG to NM)
   - Original Marvel packaging
   - Certificate of authenticity
   ```

3. **Item Photos** (Up to 5):
   
   The form shows 5 upload sections:
   ```
   📷 Top View       [Choose File]
   📷 Front View     [Choose File]
   📷 Left Side      [Choose File]
   📷 Right Side     [Choose File]
   📷 Back View      [Choose File]
   ```
   
   - Click **Choose File** for each angle
   - Select photo from your device
   - Upload happens when you submit the form

4. **Starting Price**:
   ```
   $1,500.00
   ```

5. **Bid Increment**:
   ```
   $50.00
   ```

6. **Reserve Price** (Optional):
   ```
   $3,000.00
   ```

7. **Buy Now Price** (Optional):
   ```
   $5,000.00
   ```

8. **Start Date & Time**:
   - Use the datetime picker
   - Example: December 10, 2025 at 9:00 AM

   - Example: December 17, 2025 at 9:00 PM

1. Review all information
2. Click **Create Auction**

## 📊 Managing Your Auctions

### Viewing Your Active Auctions

**From Your Dashboard:**

1. Go to **My Auctions** section
2. See a table with all your auctions:

| Title | Status | Current Bid | Bids | Actions |
|-------|--------|-------------|------|---------|
| Vintage Watch | Active | $750.00 | 12 | View \| Edit |
| Comics Collection | Active | $2,100.00 | 8 | View \| Edit |
| Camera Lens | Ended | $320.00 | 5 | View |

**Table Information:**
- **Title**: Your auction name
- **Status**: Active, Ended, Upcoming
- **Current Bid**: Highest bid so far
- **Bids**: Number of bids received
- **Actions**: Quick links to view or edit

### Monitoring Bid Activity

**Example: Your Watch Has 12 Bids**

1. Click **View** next to your auction
2. Scroll to **Recent Bids** section:

| Bidder | Amount | Time |
|--------|--------|------|
| Sarah M. | $750.00 | 2 minutes ago |
| Mike R. | $725.00 | 15 minutes ago |
| John D. | $700.00 | 1 hour ago |

**What to Look For:**
- Bidding activity level
- Current leader
- Bid progression
- Time between bids

### Editing an Active Auction

**Example: Updating the Description**

1. Go to **My Auctions**
2. Click **Edit** next to the auction
3. Make changes (description, photos, etc.)
4. **Note**: You cannot change:
   - Starting price (once bids exist)
   - End date (to make it earlier if bids exist)
   - This protects bidders

5. Save changes

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "I can't place a bid"

**Possible Causes:**

1. **Not Logged In**
   - Solution: Click "Log In" and enter your credentials
   - Look for "Please log in to place a bid" message

2. **Bid Too Low**
   - Error: "Bid must be at least $X.XX"
   - Solution: Increase bid to meet minimum requirement
   - Check the "Minimum bid" shown below the input

3. **Auction Ended**
   - Solution: Auction timer has expired, cannot bid
   - Look for similar items or wait for relisting

4. **You're the Seller**
   - Solution: You cannot bid on your own auctions
   - This prevents manipulation

5. **Technical Issue**
   - Solution: Refresh the page (Cmd/Ctrl + R)
   - Clear browser cache
   - Try a different browser

#### "My photos won't upload"

**Possible Causes:**

1. **File Too Large**
   - Solution: Resize images before uploading
   - Recommended: Under 2MB per photo
   - Use image compression tools

2. **Wrong File Type**
   - Solution: Use JPG, PNG, or GIF formats
   - Avoid TIFF, BMP, or other formats

3. **Browser Issue**
   - Solution: Update your browser
   - Try Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
   - Disable browser extensions temporarily

4. **Server Limitation**
   - Solution: Contact site administrator
   - They may need to increase upload limits

#### "I'm not receiving email notifications"

**Possible Causes:**

1. **Emails in Spam**
   - Solution: Check spam/junk folder
   - Add site email to contacts/whitelist

2. **Wrong Email Address**
   - Solution: Update email in your profile
   - Go to Profile Settings → Update Email

3. **Notifications Disabled**
   - Solution: Check your notification settings
   - Contact administrator if site-wide issue

4. **Email Server Issue**
   - Solution: Wait and check again later
   - Contact site support

#### "The auction page is blank/broken"

**Solutions:**

1. **Clear Browser Cache**:
   - Chrome: Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Delete
   - Select "Cached images and files"
   - Clear data

2. **Hard Refresh**:
   - Windows: Ctrl + F5
   - Mac: Cmd + Shift + R

3. **Try Incognito/Private Mode**:
   - Rules out extension conflicts

4. **Different Browser**:
   - Test in another browser

5. **Report to Admin**:
   - If problem persists, contact site support

#### "I can't edit my auction"

**Why This Happens:**

1. **Auction Has Bids**:
   - Can't change: Starting price, end date (earlier)
   - Can change: Description, photos, categories
   - Protects existing bidders

2. **Auction Ended**:
   - Ended auctions cannot be edited
   - Create a new auction instead

3. **Permission Issue**:
   - Verify you're logged in to the correct account
   - Only auction creator can edit

## 💡 Best Practices

### For Successful Selling

1. **Quality Photos are Essential**
   - Use all 5 photo slots
   - Good lighting, clean background
   - Show any defects honestly

2. **Write Detailed Descriptions**
   - Include all relevant details
   - Mention condition clearly
   - List what's included
   - Note any flaws or damage

3. **Price Strategically**
   - Research similar completed auctions
   - Start low to attract bidders
   - Use reserve price for protection
   - Set Buy Now 20-30% above expected final

4. **Choose Smart End Times**
   - End on weekday evenings (7-10 PM)
   - Avoid major holidays
   - Give 5-7 days for bidding

5. **Respond Promptly**
   - Answer questions quickly
   - Contact winner within 24 hours
   - Be professional and courteous

### For Successful Buying

1. **Research Before Bidding**
   - Compare similar items
   - Know market value
   - Check seller's other auctions

2. **Set a Maximum Budget**
   - Decide your limit before bidding
   - Don't get caught in bidding wars
   - Walk away if it exceeds value

3. **Bid Strategically**
   - Last-minute bidding ("sniping") can work
   - Or bid max early to discourage others
   - Use Buy Now if price is right

4. **Read Descriptions Carefully**
   - Check all photos from all angles
   - Note condition, inclusions, defects
   - Ask questions if unclear

5. **Complete Transactions Promptly**
   - Pay quickly after winning
   - Communicate clearly with seller
   - Leave feedback (if available)

### Auction Lifecycle

```
1. Creation
   ↓

   ↓
3. Bidding Period
   ↓
4. Auction Ends
   ↓
```

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|

| **Bid Increment** | Minimum increase between bids |
